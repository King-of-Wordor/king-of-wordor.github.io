it.kowd - the Italian word list of King of Wordor (RamTek)

This word list is adapted from Morph-it! by Marco Baroni and Eros Zanchetta
(https://docs.sslmit.unibo.it/doku.php?id=resources:morph-it), as shipped in
LanguageTool (https://github.com/languagetool-org/languagetool, commit
b5f37ca1c3e2c460381c932abe5de34387673883, file
languagetool-language-modules/it/src/main/resources/org/languagetool/resource/it/italian.dict).

LanguageTool's README for it: "The Italian data for part-of-speech tagging is
taken from Morph-it!, licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution
ShareAlike 2.0 License and the GNU Lesser General Public License (LGPL)".
It is used here under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 2.0
licence (CC BY-SA 2.0): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.0/

Changes made: word forms lowercased and accents folded (città is written
citta), forms tagged as proper names (NPR), abbreviations (ABL), symbols or
punctuation left out, forms longer than 15 or shorter than 3 letters left out,
and words seen in Tatoeba sentences added (written in lowercase mid-sentence
at least 3 times, not counting the part before an apostrophe), minus a
hand-checked list of English words. Word commonness comes from counts over
Tatoeba sentences (https://tatoeba.org, CC BY 2.0 FR). The file is the game's
compact sorted word-list format (KOWD).
