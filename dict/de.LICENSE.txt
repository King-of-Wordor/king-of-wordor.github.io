de.kowd - the German word list of King of Wordor (RamTek)

This word list is adapted from german-pos-dict 1.2.2 by LanguageTool
(https://github.com/languagetool-org/german-pos-dict, commit
c6dfcba71a6041e03d53c9993d65786495337416), whose data is an export from
Morphy by Wolfgang Lezius (http://morphy.wolfganglezius.de/), extended by
https://www.korrekturen.de/flexion.

Changes made: word forms lowercased and folded (ß written ss), forms tagged as
proper names (EIG) or abbreviations (ABK) left out, forms longer than 15 or
shorter than 3 letters left out, and words seen in Tatoeba sentences added
(written in lowercase mid-sentence at least 3 times, or compounds of listed
words seen at least twice), minus a hand-checked list of English words. Word
commonness comes from counts over Tatoeba sentences (https://tatoeba.org,
CC BY 2.0 FR). The file is the game's compact sorted word-list format (KOWD).
